# Public fns that look like writers (by name or by a write sink) but store no durable form of
# their own (ALICE-Law G8)
#
# scripts/provenance_writers.py: one line per candidate, `<file>::<fn> <reason>`. A reason
# `delegates to <fn>` names the covered or pending writer the body calls (through a `use ... as`
# alias is enough), and the check fails when the body stops calling it; any other reason has at
# least five words. A line that names no candidate fails.
